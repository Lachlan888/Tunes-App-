# Prompt 22 — Make Home and Social activity scroll as a feed

Read `../CONTRACT.md` and execute only the active slice.

**Source images:** image15.png, image7.png in `docs/ui-feedback/september-15/`.

**Start with:** components/activity/SocialActivityFeed.tsx; components/home/HomeFriendsActivityBox.tsx; components/friends/RecentFriendActivitySection.tsx; lib/activity-pagination.ts and app/api/activity/route.ts only if needed.

Use the same compact feed presentation on Home and Social. Give the feed a viewport-aware bounded height and its own vertical scrolling so reading more events does not move the surrounding page. Retain clear actor/tune links, timestamps, Comment and Good craic controls. Avoid large repeated empty padding. A phone feed must remain usable with touch, keyboard and expanded comments.

Reuse Prompt 19 cursor pagination, bounded reads/DOM, deduplication, visibility and loading/error/end behaviour. Bind the existing load-more trigger to the feed scroll container rather than the window; retain an accessible Load more fallback. Label the scroll region, allow keyboard scrolling and escape to adjacent page controls, and prevent unintended scroll chaining during feed scrolling without trapping the user. Preserve position during reactions/comment disclosure; do not reset the entire feed.

Slices: 22a shared container and Home; 22b Social adoption and cross-surface checks. Test multiple pages, retry, empty/end, long comments, keyboard/touch and no duplicate reactions. Record actual feed versus page scrolling at phone and laptop sizes. Do not rebuild the feed backend or introduce tracking.
