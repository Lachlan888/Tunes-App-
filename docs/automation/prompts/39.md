# Prompt 39 — Compare, beta feedback and partner festival

**Goal:** Preserve the owner's three distinct product commitments while cleaning their interaction details.

## Requirements

1. Compare remains a **first-class primary destination**. Its opening state says what outcome it finds, and choosing a person leads promptly to actual shared repertoire. Keep in-person codes, friend suggestions, privacy/consent and setlist creation intact. Do not move Compare into Social.
2. Help & feedback remains **visible at all times during beta** on user-facing screens, including focused Practice. Make it compact and non-obstructing; keep working/success/error states and useful route context. Do not hide it solely in a menu.
3. Festival is **partner activated by the app owner**. The normal app shows no festival section when mode is Off. Only the owner selects/enables a real published hub. Preserve draft/privacy rules, direct public/archived links, existing list/save/Compare reuse and no fabricated programme or automatic production activation.

## Chunks

- **P39-01 — Compare hierarchy and flow.** Improve first-screen question, participant choice, overlap summary and grouped results, with automated privacy/consent and empty/large-result tests. Keep separate primary navigation at all widths.
- **P39-02 — Persistent beta feedback.** Verify positioning on all primary routes, focused Practice, long forms, phone navigation and modal states. Fix overlap/keyboard issues while preserving visible entry. Test invocation and safe error recovery.
- **P39-03 — Festival switch and section lifecycle.** Verify owner-only selected/published/mode-on conditions, Off baseline with no reserved section, activation/deactivation, archive and safe missing-settings behavior. Fix only gaps found; leave production mode Off and use disposable data.

No deployment, festival publication or production content mutation. Each chunk writes its exact verification and successor.
