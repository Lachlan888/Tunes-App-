# Prompt 36 — Home and Practice clarity

**Goal:** Make the everyday path immediately understandable while retaining the current review method and six-destination navigation.

## Requirements

1. Home leads with one useful next action and coherent repertoire context. Avoid repeating the same count in adjacent cards or giving friend activity equal weight to today's task. Preserve Today/Repertoire/Social access and active partner-festival promotion when the owner enables it.
2. Practice directly opens/resumes the existing flow. Explain Stage as the revision interval: Stage 1–10 map to 1/2/3/7/14/30/60/90/120/360 day reviews under current rules. Show the actual due date or “due today/overdue” in human language. Remove/replace percentage-style mastery claims. Keep Rough/Shaky/Solid clear. Do not invent session goals, five-tune targets, or `3 of 8` progress framing.
3. Distinguish `Add to practice` from `Practise now`; say what happened after each action. Audit all empty, loading, saved, failed and resumed states. Do not imply a list membership is a learning commitment.
4. Help & feedback remains visibly available during beta, including focused Practice, without covering rating controls.

## Chunks

- **P36-01 — Home hierarchy and counts.** Edit Home's primary action, supporting counts/previews and copy after P35 definitions. Verify first-time, ready, overdue-only, caught-up and no-repertoire states; do not hide Compare from primary navigation.
- **P36-02 — Stage/day practice explanation.** Replace false-precision progress with Stage, interval and actual due date; explain what the three ratings do at the point of use. Test all ten stages, overdue date formatting, small screens, reduced motion and accessible names.
- **P36-03 — Journey and copy consistency.** Verify catalogue/tune → enrolment or actual practice → save rating → next tune/completion → return/resume with automated interaction and persistence checks. Confirm visible beta feedback does not overlap actions. Keep diary handoff optional under existing rules.

Use the mockups as hierarchy references, not exact pixels. Record manual visual acceptance as user-owned. Each chunk writes a result and advances the runner only on verified acceptance.
