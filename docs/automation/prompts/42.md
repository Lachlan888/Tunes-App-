# Prompt 42 — Explanatory-copy reduction

**Goal:** Reduce defensive, repeated and narrated visible copy across the whole app by communicating through hierarchy, state, graphic summaries and local disclosure while preserving truth, safety and accessibility.

## Requirements

1. Treat this as a copy/composition task independent from contextual-menu work. Do not introduce the P43 menu changes merely to make P42 pass.
2. Follow [`docs/design/COPY_AND_CONTEXT_MENUS.md`](../../design/COPY_AND_CONTEXT_MENUS.md). Remove copy that repeats headings, counts, charts, visible states or actions. Put consequences at the decision point. Explain an unfamiliar visual vocabulary once through a compact legend or information disclosure.
3. In ordinary tune collections, absence of a personal relationship has no repeated visible label. Preserve visible Known, In Practice, Stage/day, due, list, saved/shared, visibility and collaborator relationships when present.
4. Preserve explicit Stage/day intervals and dates, rating meaning at first use, privacy audience changes, Known ↔ Practice transitions, destructive consequences, contributor attribution, moderation provenance, recovery where unsaved work is at risk, form labels and accessible names.
5. Replace narrated data with literal graphic treatment where useful: schedule rail/annotations, audience marks, Diary/Trends legends and charts, readiness distributions and festival lifecycle states. Do not introduce mastery percentages or decorative symbols without meaning.
6. Audit all user-facing route states: ready, loading, empty, error, filtered, selected, editing, modal/sheet and role-gated variants. Mobile, tablet and desktop retain the same meaning and actions.

## Chunks

- **P42-01 — Tune collection and detail copy.** Remove null relationship repetition and duplicate result copy in catalogue/repertoire/trend/profile tune collections. Consolidate Tune Info personal-state and provenance explanations. Give empty Reference one message and one action while suppressing downstream empty sections. Preserve Stage/day, attribution, actions and accessible labels.
- **P42-02 — Everyday and social copy.** Reduce repeated description across shell/account, Home, Practice, Lists, Compare, Social, Inbox, profiles and settings. Keep primary purpose, relationship, privacy and transition truth visible. Use one useful empty state and one recovery/action per region.
- **P42-03 — Secondary workspaces and copy gate.** Apply the copy rules to Diary, Focus, Trends, Setlists, Badges, Festival, moderation, developer, auth and global loading/error states. Add literal legends/graphic summaries where they replace prose. Run an app-wide copy inventory and responsive rendered check; record remaining owner acceptance separately.

Do not delete working behaviour or hide information to create visual quiet. Each chunk records affected copy, preserved safety text, checks and the next item.
